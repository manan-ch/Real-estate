Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers on buying, selling, luxury financing, and off-market enclaves across Scottsdale and Paradise Valley.

How do I schedule a private showing for an exclusive estate in Scottsdale or Paradise Valley?
To preserve homeowner confidentiality and secure gated enclave protocols in Silverleaf, Paradise Valley, and Desert Mountain, showings require an advance request. You can click "Schedule Showing" on any property card, or reach our concierge desk directly at +1 (480) 922-8400. For estates exceeding $5M, a customary proof of funds or private banking pre-approval letter is requested prior to private walkthroughs.
What are off-market pocket listings and how does MERIDIAN access them?
Approximately 25% of high-net-worth real estate transactions in Paradise Valley and North Scottsdale occur off-market without public MLS exposure. As active licensed members of the Scottsdale Area Association of REALTORS® (SAAR) and private Arizona luxury broker networks, MERIDIAN maintains private access to discreet offerings. Registered clients receive confidential dossier presentations before estates ever appear in public searches.
What financing options and down payment terms apply to Arizona luxury homes?
Transactions above $2,000,000 typically utilize Jumbo or Super-Jumbo portfolio financing. Arizona luxury lenders generally require 15% to 20% down with 6 to 12 months in verified reserves. MERIDIAN works closely with premier private wealth institutions offering competitive 30-year fixed, interest-only ARM, and securities-backed credit lines. You can model estimated monthly investments on our Mortgage Calculator page.
How do Maricopa County property taxes compare to California or New York?
Arizona offers one of the lowest effective property tax structures in the United States, typically between 0.55% and 0.65% of assessed market value across Maricopa County. By comparison, luxury buyers relocating from California, Illinois, or New York often save tens of thousands of dollars annually. For example, a $6,000,000 Scottsdale residence generally incurs approximately $24,000 to $32,000 in annual property taxes.
What is the process for obtaining a complimentary Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)?
Our senior partners prepare bespoke Comparative Market Analyses that analyze hyper-local micro-trends, recent enclave sales, view elevation premiums, custom architectural finishes, and current inventory absorption rates. Visit our Sell with MERIDIAN page to request a confidential valuation, or contact our Scottsdale office directly.
What broker fees or representation agreements apply under new NAR rules?
In full accordance with the 2024 National Association of REALTORS® (NAR) guidelines, buyer broker representation agreements outline representation terms and fee arrangements prior to touring properties. In many luxury Arizona transactions, sellers continue to provide buyer broker concessions or agreed cooperative fees at closing. We review all terms transparently during your initial discovery session.
